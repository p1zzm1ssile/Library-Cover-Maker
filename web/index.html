Library Cover Maker

Add up to 5 videos or pictures, or pick a folder and let it choose 5 at random. Type your library name and get an animated WebP or GIF cover that cuts between moments from each one, or a still PNG. Switch to Poster for a portrait film or series poster. Choose the moment for each video, or leave it random. Pictures get a slow zoom. Connected to Jellyfin or Emby, it can cut the moments from a title’s own video and make one for every film, series and library in one go.

Drag to move the picture. Scroll or use the Zoom slider to resize it. Click a file in the list to preview it. Each video has its own slider for choosing the moment.

File size settings

Smaller sizes, fewer frames and fewer colours make smaller files. The moments setting is how many moments are cut from a film, episodes from a series, or titles from a library when the pictures come from your server.

  3. Add the CSS from the chat for that library, then press Ctrl+F5.

    Posters for every film and series

    Animated posters for your whole library are not recommended on Fire TV sticks, smart TVs and other low-end devices. They have a small processor and little memory, and a screen full of animated posters can make them slow, stutter or crash. Animated library covers are fine, as there are only a few of them. For posters on every film and series, choose Still PNG, or try a few animated ones by hand on your slowest device first.
